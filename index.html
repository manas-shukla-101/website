WELCOME TO

Thakur Shyamnarayan Degree College (TSDC)

Thakur Shyamnarayan Degree College (TSDC) was established in the academic year 2016 and is approved by the Government of Maharashtra and affiliated to the University of Mumbai. The College complies with all norms and standards prescribed by the University of Mumbai.

Thakur Shyamnarayan Degree College (TSDC), within a short span, has achieved several notable milestones across various academic and co-curricular domains. The College is committed to setting high standards in the field of education. Skill Development, Entrepreneurial Expertise, and Industry Collaboration are among the key initiatives undertaken to provide the best opportunities for learners and their mentors.
